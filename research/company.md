# BeeD Consulting sh.p.k. / "BeeD Energy": public footprint

Researched 2026-10-23. Every line below has its source and the date it was read. Anything not confirmed is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## Summary

- **The company exists and is active.** The Albanian business registry (QKB) holds one entity with "BeeD" in its legal name: **BeeD Consulting shpk, NIPT M41722050A**. It is a limited-liability company (SHPK) registered in Tirana on 22/05/2024, and its status is **Aktiv**.
- **"BeeD Energy" is not a registered name.** The QKB has no entity or trade name "BeeD Energy". The brand appears only as the handwritten label on the brief, so it is the platform or brand name, not the legal name.
- **The company has almost no public footprint.** No website, LinkedIn company page, Facebook or Instagram page, or Google Maps listing could be confirmed. No news, directory or tender mention was found either. One domain, `beedconsulting.com`, was registered on 2026-08-07 and shows a Squarespace "Coming Soon" page. Its owner is unknown.
- **Name-collision risk is low in the energy sector but real for the word "Bee".** No "BeeD" trademark exists in Albania, the Western Balkans, the EU (EUIPO) or WIPO. The closest names are several "Bee…" companies in Albania (one of them in construction) and an Albanian word mark **"Bee" in Nice class 35**. A trademark attorney should look at that mark before "BeeD Energy" is filed.

## 6. Needs confirmation from the client

1. **Brand vs legal name.** Should the site be branded "BeeD Energy", with "BeeD Consulting sh.p.k." in the legal footer? Confirm the exact capitalisation (BeeD, not Beed or BEED) and whether there is a logo.
2. **Address to publish.** The registered office is a residential unit (address in the registry, withheld here). Ask whether to show this address or another business or correspondence address. Albania's e-commerce law (Law No. 10128/2009, https://wilmap.stanford.edu/node/29700) sets information duties for online service providers; the exact list of what the footer must show is **UNVERIFIED** and is for the client's lawyer.
3. **Business contacts.** The only contacts in the registry are the administrator's personal e-mail and mobile. Ask for a business e-mail (ideally on the platform's domain), a phone number, and opening hours if buyers can visit or return goods.
4. **Administrator's name.** The registry lists it, but per BRIEF.md it is **not for publication** unless the client confirms.
5. **Domains.** Does the client own `beedconsulting.com`, registered 2026-08-07 on Squarespace? Which domain should the platform use? `beedenergy.com` and `beed-energy.com` are unregistered, and `beedenergy.al` has no DNS (probably free). Buy before launch.
6. **Social accounts.** None were found. Ask whether any exist or are planned, so the footer links are real.
7. **VAT (TVSH) status.** Not checked; the tax registry was unreachable. It affects how checkout shows prices (with or without TVSH) and the invoice fields.
8. **Registered object vs marketplace.** Is the current object, through its catch-all clause, enough for operating an online marketplace and taking commissions, or does the client plan to update it at the QKB?
9. **Share-capital currency.** The extract shows 1.000.000,00 without a currency. Only needed if the capital is published; it usually is not.
10. **Company track record.** There are no public projects, clients, team size or certifications. Any such claim on the site (years of experience, projects delivered, partners or sellers onboarded) must come from the client, with proof.
11. **Funding programme.** The brief is a page from a project application form signed 2026-06-09. Which programme or donor is it, and does the site need "co-funded by" logos or a disclaimer?
12. **Trademark.** There is no "BeeD" mark in Albania. Suggest that the client discuss filing "BeeD Energy" with the Drejtoria e Përgjithshme e Pronësisë Industriale (DPPI/GDIP), in classes 35, 37 and 42 for example, after an attorney has looked at the existing "Bee" class-35 mark.
