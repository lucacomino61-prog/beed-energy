# BeeD Energy — build plan

Brief: `research/BRIEF.md`. Design: `DESIGN.md` (house in layers, picked by Luca 2026-10-23). Architecture: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
Local only: dev server http://127.0.0.1:3810 (launch entry "beed-energy", `DEV_LOGIN=1`). **Nothing is deployed or pushed.**

## Rules for every task
1. **Files you own** are listed per task. Shared files (`src/styles/{tokens,base,global}.css`, `src/layouts/Site.astro`, `src/components/{Header,Menu,Footer,Dock,Tray,Loader,BrandMark}.astro`, `src/scripts/{motion,site}.ts`, `src/lib/*.ts`, `src/i18n/common.ts`) are read-only for builders: if you need a change there, describe it in your report. You may add new `src/lib/<area>-*.ts` helpers.
2. **Routes are thin**: `src/pages/<sq-path>.astro` and `src/pages/en/<en-path>.astro` render one shared view in `src/views/<Name>.astro` with `lang`. Build every link with `href(lang, key, params, query)`; pass `alternate` (the same page in the other language) to `<Site>`.
3. **Words**: every visible word in Albanian and English in `src/i18n/<area>.ts` (`{ sq: {...}, en: {...} }`). Check terms against `research/glossary-sq-en.md`. Albanian with ë and ç, second person "ju". No invented facts: numbers come from `research/*.md` with their source; demo sellers/prices come only from the seed (`is_demo = 1`) and show a "shembull / sample" tag.
4. **Server first**: words, prices and links are in the HTML; forms work with scripts off (POST to the page or an `/api/*` route, PRG redirect, errors next to fields with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`, first error focused). Scripts only enhance.
5. **Design**: `DESIGN.md` + classes in `global.css` (`.wrap .grid-12 .sheet-head .titleblock .btn .tag .price .table .pcard .field .input .select .choice .qty .notice .empty .eyebrow .poster .title`). Ink, copper, paper only. Sharp corners. Page CSS goes in `src/styles/<area>.css` imported by your views.
6. **Motion**: only through `src/scripts/motion.ts` (`gsap`, `ScrollTrigger`, `motionOn()`, `onMotionChange()`). Never a second `requestAnimationFrame`, never another animation library. Nothing moves when `html[data-motion=off]`. No motion over forms or number entry. Entrances never move tap targets (opacity only).
7. **Accessibility**: one h1, landmarks, labels on every control, 44 px touch targets, visible focus, state never by colour alone, tables with `<th scope>`.
8. **Verify your own pages** against the running dev server (do not start another server, do not use the Browser pane): `curl` for status and content; screenshots with `node <session scratchpad>/shot.mjs <url> <out.png> <w> <h> [1=fullpage]` at 1440×900 and 390×844 (save PNGs in the scratchpad, look at them); then the gate `node ~/.claude/skills/awwwards-blueprints/scripts/audit.mjs <url>` run from the scratchpad folder: fix every FAIL. Finish with `npx astro check` (0 errors in your files). Do not commit; the director commits.

## Acceptance criteria (the whole site)
- Every public page exists in sq and en with hreflang pairs, one h1, title 25–65 chars, description 70–160 chars.
- Home: loader ≤ 1.6 s once per session with skip; poster first screen with the way in (shop, quote); the house opens in 7 layers, each with a sourced fact and its category's live count and lowest price; reduced motion shows the static exploded house + list.
- Shop: 14 categories; category pages filter by every filterable spec (ranges and sets, counts shown, state in the URL), sort by price / price per unit; list view toggle; cards show drawing, key specs, lowest price, offer count, unit price.
- Product: drawing with real dimensions + title block, full spec table, datasheet link, offers table (seller, verified, rating, price incl. TVSH, unit price, volume tiers, stock, lead time, delivery to the chosen city, total delivered), sorted by total; "best price" and "fastest" badges; add any offer to the basket; price history line (90 days) with a table alternative; price alert form; compare toggle; Product JSON-LD with AggregateOffer.
- Compare: up to 4 products of one category, comparable specs side by side, best value marked, "show differences only", lowest price and unit price rows.
- Basket: lines grouped by seller (one parcel each), qty steppers, problems explained (stock, min qty, zone), delivery per parcel for the chosen city, VAT contained, totals.
- Checkout: individual or business (company + NIPT validated), Albanian phone, city list, courier or pickup, payment: cash on delivery (≤ limit), bank transfer, card (POK or the labelled test gateway); terms consent; order created in one transaction; order page with timeline, parcels, payment instructions.
- Admin: sign-in (DEV_LOGIN button on 127.0.0.1); dashboard; orders with status changes and timeline; products with the spec form generated from the category template; categories with the spec-template editor (add/remove/reorder attributes, types, units, flags); sellers; offers (full edit + quick price/stock edit, tiers, price history); price monitor; quote threads with replies; seller applications (approve); contact messages; settings (delivery, bank, contact, tariffs, plans). Seller role sees only its own data.
- Calculator: city (PVGIS), monthly kWh or bill, household/business, battery, phases → kWp, panels, yearly kWh by month, saving with the ERE cliff, kit priced from live offers, payback; every assumption and source shown; "add the kit to the basket".
- audit.mjs: 0 FAIL on every route at 1440/390/320; CLS < 0.1; no horizontal overflow; JS-off pages still show words, prices and working forms.
